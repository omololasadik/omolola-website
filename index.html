About

Communications strategist, backed by research

I work at the intersection of communications, growth marketing, and policy research. Over the past two years, I have managed editorial calendars and content strategy for six or more clients across health, technology, SaaS, and education, and led a 4-person B2B outreach team, personally reaching over 150 HR leaders and executives while each team member carried a 50+ weekly contact target. I have also built media partnerships that secured sponsored coverage for a growth-stage HR-tech platform, led a four-person editorial team, and launched a newsletter that grew its own readership from the ground up.

My academic background is in Philosophy, where I graduated First Class and was named Best Graduating Student out of a class of 66 at the University of Ibadan. That training in argument construction, critical analysis, and evidence-based reasoning shapes my research work: I led field interviews with 31 market women and the market association leader in Egbeda LGA, coded the data myself, and authored a policy paper on informal labour exclusion now headed toward policy and civil actors.

I bring the same rigour and focus to content and marketing projects as I do to policy projects: understand the audience, break down the project, research thoroughly, and communicate with precision.

Process

My Approach

01
Observe
I pay attention before I act. I look for patterns in audience behaviour, lived experiences, stakeholder feedback, research findings, and market signals to understand what's really happening.
↓
02
Analyse
I question assumptions, identify root causes, and connect ideas that others might treat separately. My philosophy training taught me to break down concepts and think deeply about problems before I recommend solutions.
↓
03
Communicate
The primary purpose of communication for me is clarity and understanding. Whether I'm writing a thought article on LinkedIn, a research paper, a policy brief, a search engine optimized article, or a stakeholder report, I turn complex ideas into language my target audience can understand and act on.
↓
04
Learn and Improve
I treat every project as feedback. Performance data, interviews, and stakeholder responses inform the next iteration until the work achieves its purpose. Every project leaves behind insights that make the next one stronger.
